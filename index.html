      ⚠️ Important Details & Edge Cases

      Both options earn Bilt Points on everyday spend

      A common misconception: Option 2 does not replace your spend points with Bilt Cash — it adds 4% Bilt Cash on top of your regular card-rate Bilt Points. This makes Option 2 almost always superior for high spenders.

      Bilt Cash → rent points conversion rate

      $3 Bilt Cash = 100 Bilt Points on rent (max 1× on rent). Any Bilt Cash beyond what's needed to reach 1× on rent becomes a statement credit or can be saved for later use within the Bilt ecosystem.

      Option 2: you choose how much Bilt Cash to use

      You're not forced to convert any Bilt Cash to rent points. You can use any amount — from $0.03 (unlocks 1 point) up to the full 1× on rent — or keep all of it as statement credit for other Bilt ecosystem uses.

      Option 2: no minimum points floor on housing

      Option 1 guarantees at least 250 Bilt Points on rent even if you barely use your card. Option 2 has no floor — if you don't redeem any Bilt Cash, you earn 0 points on rent.

      Switch options monthly (with one billing cycle delay)

      You can change your Rewards Option before the last day of any billing cycle — the change takes effect on the first day of the next billing cycle. Plan ahead for big-spend or high-rent months.

      Housing payments must go through the Bilt App or website

      Neither option applies if you pay rent outside the Bilt platform. You must also have a valid bank account connected to your Bilt Rewards Account to earn any housing rewards under either option.

      Bilt Obsidian: choose dining OR grocery (locks for the year)

      You get 3× on one category. The selection defaults to Dining and can be changed once within 30 days of your account opening (or of February 7, 2026 for older accounts). After that it locks for the calendar year. The other category earns 1×.

      Bilt Obsidian: $25,000/year grocery cap

      If you choose Grocery as your 3× bonus category, the 3× rate applies up to $25,000 in combined grocery spend per calendar year. Anything beyond that earns 1× for the rest of the year.

      Bilt Palladium: additional annual benefits not in this calculator

      The Palladium card includes $200 Bilt Cash annually (Jan 1 each year), $400 in hotel credits ($200 per half-year via Bilt Travel Portal), and a 50,000-point welcome bonus (with $4,000 spend in 90 days). These are substantial offsets against the $495 fee.

      ⚡ Point Accelerator — boost your earning (Option 2 only)

      Obsidian & Palladium cardholders on Option 2 can redeem $200 Bilt Cash to activate a Point Accelerator: +1× Bilt Point per $1 on eligible purchases (stacks on top of card base rates), up to $5,000 in purchases per activation (= up to 5,000 bonus pts). Activations: up to 5×/year. Rent, housing fees, and tax payments are excluded. Point Accelerator ends immediately if you switch to Option 1.

      Bilt Cash expires at end of calendar year ($100 rolls over)

      Bilt Cash earned in a calendar year expires on Dec 31 of that year. Only $100 of Bilt Cash rolls over to the following year (used on a first-in, first-out basis). Use your balance before year-end — converting to rent points or activating a Point Accelerator are both valid ways to preserve value.

      Bilt Cash is NOT a statement credit — it stays in the Bilt ecosystem

      Bilt Cash cannot be redeemed as a direct card statement credit or withdrawn as cash. It can only be used within the Bilt platform: rent point conversion ($3 = 100 pts), Point Accelerator activations (Obsidian/Palladium), Lyft/Walgreens/Grubhub monthly credits ($10 each), hotel bookings via Bilt Travel Portal, or program tier upgrades.
